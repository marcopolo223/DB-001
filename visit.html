A few practical things

Small room, busy mornings, easy to miss if you only look at the street.

  • We sit in a business plaza. When the spots out front fill up, loop the buildings — there is more parking than it looks.
  • Casual everything. Come from the airport, the boat, or a job site.
  • Vegetarian plates and a few lighter options are on the menu. Ask about egg whites or the veggie omelette.
  • Credit cards are fine. Coffee refills are part of the deal.
