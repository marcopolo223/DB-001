Since 1993

A diner, not a concept.

Robert's is the unassuming room in a Dania Beach plaza where the coffee keeps coming, the hash browns actually crunch, and half the room already knows what they want.

How it feels

Wooden chairs, a long counter, and a board that changes with the morning.

There is no theme restaurant happening here. Beige walls, fish sculptures on the back wall, cake stands on the granite, and a television in the corner. The room fills up with families, cops on a break, and people who have been coming long enough that the staff does not need to ask.

  • Home-style cooking, made fresh daily — guests notice when nothing comes out of a bag.
  • Breakfast and lunch only. We open at 6 and we are done at 3.
  • Takeout is part of the rhythm. The pass is stacked with cups and tickets for a reason.
  • A member of the Greater Dania Beach Chamber of Commerce.

From the pass

The kitchen still cooks like a kitchen.

Order slips hang on the rail. Plates go out hot. Cornbread, chili, hash, soups, and the daily turkey are the kind of work you taste. People come for a Western omelette or a Reuben and stay because the staff works the room like a team that has been together a long time — some of the same faces for twenty years.
